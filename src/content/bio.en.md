I am a systems neuroscience Research Fellow in the [Erlich Lab](https://erlichlab.org/) at the Sainsbury Wellcome Centre, University College London. I completed my PhD at UCL in April 2026.

Outside the lab, I enjoy road cycling: exploring new routes and scenery, and climbing mountains under my own power. You can follow my rides on [Strava](https://www.strava.com/athletes/145556491).

## My PhD work

During my PhD, I studied how the brain flexibly selects, maintains, and transforms information to guide decisions and actions. My work combines carefully designed behavioural tasks for rats with Neuropixels recordings, optogenetics, and quantitative modelling.

## Future direction

I am actively seeking a postdoctoral position. I am particularly interested in motor control, short-term memory, and decision-making, and in understanding embodied cognition: how we use the body as a resource for cognition when solving tasks, and the neural mechanisms that make this possible. Please [reach out](mailto:jingjie.li.21@ucl.ac.uk) if you would like to have a conversation.
